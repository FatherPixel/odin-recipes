Chicken Parmesan

Home

Description

Chicken parmesan (also called chicken parmigiana or chicken parm) is an Italian-American dish made of breaded chicken cutlets topped with tomato sauce and melted cheese.

Ingredients

  • Boneless chicken breasts or cutlets
  • flour
  • egg
  • breadcrumbs (plain, Italian-seasoned, or panko)
  • marinara or tomato sauce
  • mozzarella cheese
  • grated Parmesan or provolone

Steps

  1. Prep the chicken: Slice chicken breasts into thin cutlets and pound them to an even thickness (about ¼ to ½ inch) so they cook evenly. Season with salt and pepper.
  2. Bread the cutlets: Set up three shallow bowls—seasoned flour, whisked eggs, and breadcrumbs (often mixed with grated Parmesan). Coat each cutlet in flour, dip in egg, and press into the breadcrumb mixture.
  3. Brown or bake: Pan-fry in oil until golden brown, or bake on a lightly oiled/lined pan until crisp.
  4. Top and melt: Spoon tomato or marinara sauce over the chicken, top with mozzarella and Parmesan, and bake or broil until the chicken reaches 165°F (74°C) and the cheese is melted and bubbly.
