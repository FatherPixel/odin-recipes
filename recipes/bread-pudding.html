Bread Pudding

Home

Description

Bread pudding is a baked dish made from stale or day-old bread soaked in a liquid custard and baked until set. Sweet versions are served as desserts or brunch, while savory versions—such as breakfast strata—serve as main courses.

Ingredients

  • Bread: Day-old or slightly stale bread works best because it absorbs liquid without turning mushy. Brioche, challah, French bread, sourdough, or standard sandwich loaves are common choices.
  • Custard base: Eggs, milk, heavy cream (or half-and-half), sugar, and melted butter.
  • Flavorings & add-ins: Cinnamon, nutmeg, vanilla extract, raisins, dried fruit, nuts, or chocolate chips.

Steps

  1. Prep the bread: Cut or tear bread into bite-sized cubes or slices, and place them in a greased baking dish.
  2. Soak: Whisk the eggs, dairy, sugar, butter, and spices into a custard. Pour it over the bread and let it soak for 10 to 30 minutes so the center absorbs the liquid.
  3. Bake: Bake at around 350°F (175°C) for 30 to 50 minutes until golden on top and set in the center.
  4. Enjoy warm or at room temperature with vanilla sauce, caramel or butter sauce, whiskey/bourbon sauce, whipped cream, or ice cream.
